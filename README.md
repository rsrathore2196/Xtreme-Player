## ✨ Key Features

### 🎧 High-Fidelity Audio Streaming
- **True 320 kbps HQ Audio:** Stream crystal-clear, high-bitrate music (MP3, AAC, FLAC) with minimal compression[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
- **Smart Caching & Zero Buffering:** Intelligent pre-caching ensures uninterrupted playback even on unstable connections[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
- **Background Playback & Media Controls:** Persistent Android 13+ lock screen and notification controls powered by AndroidX Media3[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
- **Music & Playlists:** Import playlists from Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, or custom local files[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
- **Better Recommendations:** Cleaner regional song recommendations without repetitive country tags[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
- **Fresh Genre Playlists:** Fixed shuffle logic to load new, randomized songs every time you open a genre[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

### 🎨 Clean & Modern Material UI
- **Material 3 Design:** Sleek, minimalistic aesthetic with dynamic AMOLED dark mode or Material light mode and fluid animations[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
- **Modern Full-Screen Player:** Ambient artwork glow, dynamic color palette matching, real-time waveform scrubbing, and tactile haptic controls[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
- **Persistent Mini-Player:** Floating mini-player with swipe-to-skip gestures and seamless bottom-sheet expansion[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
- **Visuals & Customization:**
  - **10 Curated Themes:** Added 5 Dark and 5 Light presets with full color palette controls[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
  - **Granular Color Picker:** Customize Accent, Canvas, and Card colors with live swatches and weight options[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
  - **Custom Gradients:** Fine-tune Background, Card, and Bottom Sheet gradients with new visual presets[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
  - **Text & UI Scaling:** Flexible 7-level scaling options for both typography and full interface density[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).

### 🔍 Discovery & Music Management
- **Instant Online Search:** Fast, debounced search engine for tracks, artists, top hits, and albums[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).
- **Personalized Library:** Save favorites, organize custom playlists, and view listening history[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).
- **Queue & Playlists:** Drag-and-drop track reordering, shuffle modes, and smart loop controls[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).

### 🔓 100% Free & Open Source
- **No Ads, No Paywalls:** Pure music streaming without sponsored interruptions[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).
- **Privacy-First:** No invasive tracking, profiling, or unnecessary background permissions[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
- **Community-Driven:** Fully open-source codebase open for contributions and audits[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).

## 🛠 Built With

- **Language:** Kotlin[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span)
- **UI Toolkit:** Jetpack Compose & Material Design 3[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span)
- **Audio Engine:** AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer + MediaSessionService)[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span)
- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture + MVI / MVVM[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span)
- **Database:** Room (Local playlists and metadata caching)[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span)
- **Image Loading:** Coil Compose[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span)
- **Networking:** Retrofit2 & OkHttp3[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)
